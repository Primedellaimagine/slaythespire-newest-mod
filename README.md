## 📜 Overview

**Slay the Spire 2 Toolkit 2026** is a companion project concept for players who want to improve their roguelike strategy, organize deck ideas, track relic combinations, and review their ascents through the Spire.

Plan card synergies, document character builds, compare route choices, and keep notes on encounters that challenge your strategy.

Whether you're experimenting with a new archetype, preparing for an elite encounter, or coordinating a co-op run with friends, this toolkit is designed to keep your strategic notes organized.

The game entered Early Access on March 5, 2026, featuring new and returning characters, additional cards and relics, and online co-op for up to four players. Content and balance may change during Early Access. See the [official announcement](https://www.megacrit.com/news/2026-03-05-early-access-launch/).

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Open the game** — Launch Slay the Spire 2 through Steam.
2. **Choose a character** — Start a run and note your initial strategy.
3. **Plan your deck** — Record cards, upgrades, and synergies worth pursuing.
4. **Review the map** — Compare available paths and potential rewards.
5. **Track relics** — Note how relic effects influence your strategy.
6. **Review encounters** — Record difficult fights and useful lessons.
7. **Analyze the run** — Evaluate your choices after victory or defeat.

*The steps above describe a manual companion workflow, not an installer or a verified in-game integration.*

## 🃏 Deck Building & Synergy Lab

Build a coherent strategy instead of collecting cards without a plan.

- **Archetype Notes** — Record themes such as multi-hit damage, defensive scaling, summons, or status effects when supported by your chosen character.
- **Core Cards** — Identify cards that your planned strategy relies on.
- **Upgrade Priorities** — Track which upgrades could improve consistency or efficiency.
- **Energy Management** — Consider the energy cost of cards you expect to play together.
- **Deck Consistency** — Review whether the deck has enough defense, damage, and scaling for later encounters.
- **Adaptation Notes** — Record when an unexpected reward suggests changing your original plan.

## 💎 Relic & Item Tracker

Understand how your collected items influence the run.

- Record relics obtained during each ascent.
- Document synergies with your current deck.
- Compare immediate rewards with longer-term benefits.
- Note relics that change your preferred combat approach.
- Track potions and consumable-resource decisions.
- Keep notes on combinations you want to test in future runs.

## 🗺️ Map Route & Resource Planner

Plan a route that fits your deck's current strengths and weaknesses.

- Compare available paths before committing.
- Evaluate the potential value of shops and campfires.
- Consider whether your deck is prepared for elite fights.
- Record healing and upgrade priorities.
- Plan alternative routes when your health or resources are low.
- Document the reasoning behind major route decisions.

## ⚔️ Combat & Enemy Reference

Create a personal strategy notebook for difficult battles.

- Record enemy moves and attack patterns observed during play.
- Review damage thresholds and defensive requirements.
- Plan when to use potions or other limited resources.
- Document dangerous combinations of enemies.
- Track which cards were most useful in each encounter.
- Record adjustments for future attempts.

*Enemy behavior, card effects, and balance can change as Early Access develops. Keep notes tied to the game version you played.*

## 👥 Character Build Manager

Maintain a separate strategy profile for each playable character.

- Record character-specific mechanics.
- Organize preferred card packages.
- Track useful relic combinations.
- Compare defensive and offensive approaches.
- Document which builds succeeded or struggled.
- Keep experimental strategies separate from reliable favorites.

## 🤝 Co-op Strategy Planner

Coordinate with friends in multiplayer runs.

- Discuss team roles before difficult encounters.
- Record ways characters can complement each other's decks.
- Coordinate resource use and combat priorities.
- Plan how to support a teammate who needs healing or protection when the game allows it.
- Review team decisions after each run.
- Keep a shared strategy journal for future sessions.

## 📊 Run Statistics & Performance Review

Turn each attempt into useful experience.

- Record character, difficulty, and run outcome.
- Track major boss encounters and run milestones.
- Note the deck's strengths and weaknesses.
- Compare route decisions and resource management.
- Identify recurring mistakes.
- Set one or two strategic goals for the next ascent.

All statistics can be recorded manually. Automatic collection of game data is not claimed.

## 💾 Presets & Strategy Profiles

Keep your most useful ideas organized.

- Save reusable deck-planning templates.
- Maintain separate solo and co-op strategy notes.
- Catalog card and relic interactions to investigate.
- Keep a list of unanswered mechanics questions.
- Organize experimental builds by character.
- Record the game version associated with each strategy.
